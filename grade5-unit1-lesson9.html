GRADE 5 · UNIT 1 · LESSON 9

Music

Name musical instruments, talk about plans with be going to, read about a band and about music around the world, and write about what you are going to do. Source material is separated by book and kept in original page order.

Pupil’s Book

Pages 30–32 · instrument words, the band picture, the “MJ” reading and be going to.

Workbook

Pages 31–33 · spelling correction, the music reading, listening tasks and Lily’s list.

Vocabulary & Explanation

Instruments, music words and be going to, with Arabic meanings.

Lesson Test

18 independent questions and a separate teacher-answer-key area.

Learning goals

  • Name musical instruments.
  • Read and understand a short text about a band and a concert.
  • Talk about plans with am / is / are going to.
  • Make negative sentences with isn’t / aren’t going to.
  • Use time words for the future: tonight, tomorrow, next week, in the evening.
  • Write sentences about what you and other people are going to do.

Every exercise on the supplied book pages can be answered on screen. Where the book prints an answer, that answer is shown and marked as printed. Where the book prints no key, the exercise stores your answer and shows a suggested answer only where the page itself settles it. Listening tasks keep every printed instruction, picture and option, but no answer is invented and no audio file has been created.

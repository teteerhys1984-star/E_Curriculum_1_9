Question 13 of 18 · Multiple choice

13. Read the text. Why did Karim plan his machine?

Karim’s Helpful Machine

A short original passage written only for this assessment.

Karim is eleven years old and he lives in a small town near the sea. Last year, Karim saw that his grandmother carried heavy water from the well to the kitchen every morning. He wanted to help her, so he planned a simple machine. He drew many pictures first. Then he tried three times. The first two machines broke, but Karim did not stop. He fixed the wheels and made the box stronger, and he carried the machine to the well. This time it worked, and his grandmother smiled. Today, other families in the town have the same machine. Karim did not become famous, but his neighbours remember his idea. “A small idea can change a long day,” he says.

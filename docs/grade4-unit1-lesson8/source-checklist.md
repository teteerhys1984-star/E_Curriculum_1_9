## Pupil’s Book content inventory

### Page 27 (image 7)

1. **1 · Listen and tick** — four printed pairs of place pictures: bank with post office, hospital with restaurant, supermarket with library, bakery with school. **Audio-dependent: unavailable**; no tick answers are invented.
2. **2 · Listen, point and repeat** — six printed place pictures with printed numbers `1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 4`. **Source defect:** the number `4` is printed on both Bakery and School. Both are preserved exactly and are not treated as an unambiguous answer key. **Audio-dependent: unavailable** for repetition.

## Source limitations

- No audio or transcript was supplied. PB exercises 1–2 (listening/tick and repetition) and WB exercise 6 are explicitly unavailable for audio verification. The pages show printed prompts only and never fabricate recordings, transcripts, tick answers or box numbers.
- The PB p.27 exercise 2 duplicated printed number `4` for Bakery and School is preserved verbatim and is not used as a unique answer.
- Map and picture placements are image-derived inferences, consistently distinguished from printed answer keys.
- No official answer key was supplied for the textbook exercises. Open and personal prompts show labelled illustrative examples, not unique answers.
- Arabic appears only on the Vocabulary page as editorial learner-support glosses; it is not printed textbook translation.

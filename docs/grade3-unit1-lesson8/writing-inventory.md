## Writing standard applied

- Exercises 1, 2, 3, 5 and 6 are managed by `lesson8-writing-practice.js`: drafts saved in the browser, a labelled model shown on request, comparison that never overwrites the pupil's text, and reset for that exercise only.
- Exercises 3 and 6 are open. The comparison says answers vary and the examples are illustrative, not official answers.
- Exercise 1 is matching, so it is managed as a selection with an inferred model; the pictures are numbered 1–4 for this exercise only (editorial). It is not counted as a writing activity.
- Exercise 4 is listening and is not scored.
- No score is shown for any Workbook exercise.

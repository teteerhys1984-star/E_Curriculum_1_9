## Standard applied

- Each writing activity is a separate `data-writing-activity` group on its source page with response entry, local draft persistence, an activity-specific reset and, where the source supports one, a labelled model. Revealing a model never overwrites the pupil’s draft.
- Models derived from printed word order or passage meaning are labelled **inferred**, not official answer keys. The one worked example in PB exercise 8 is labelled **printed model**.
- Open and personal prompts show labelled illustrative examples where answers vary; no unique answer or automatic score is claimed.
- The audio-dependent PB exercises 1, 2 and pronunciation and WB exercises 3–4 remain model-free and are explicitly disclosed.
- The PB p.25 exercise 6b kilometre mismatch is disclosed on the page; the conflicting item is not scored and is not used as a writing model.
- Nothing in the textbook exercises is autograded. The independent Lesson Test is a separate assessment with its own 12 authored items and teacher key.

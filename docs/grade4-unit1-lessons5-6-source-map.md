## Source notes and limitations

- Titles transcribed from the scans: Lesson 5 “School Subjects”; Lesson 6 “Where I Live”.
- Workbook listening activities are visible on Lesson 5 pages 17–18 and Lesson 6 pages 22–23. No audio or transcript was supplied, so no listening transcript or answer key has been made.
- Lesson 5’s Revision 1 source prints forms including “There is two beds and a wardrobe in my bedroom”, “Lily always wake up at 7 o’clock”, and “Sally is brown eyes”. These remain as printed in the source scan; they are not silently corrected.
- Lesson 5 Workbook p. 20 contains a pair of alternatives for several grammar items; the original scan is retained exactly. The scan remains the authoritative exercise where the layout or underlining makes the intended correction dependent on visual inspection.
- Lesson 5 Project p. 20 has identity-card blanks and learner-created clock designs. They are kept as open-ended source work, not given fabricated personal answers.
- No crop was needed: source images are legible at responsive page width. Zero crops are recorded and no image has been edited, resized or recompressed.

# Source defects and unresolved items — Grade 5, Unit 1, Lesson 8 “Inventions”

Every item below was seen in the supplied scans and is handled in one of three ways. The lesson never silently corrects, completes or reinterprets the printed page, and it never invents an answer where the page does not settle one.

Legend:

- **Reproduced** — the printed string is kept exactly, and a note on the page says what is odd about it.
- **Unscored** — the activity is answerable on screen but nothing is marked correct, because the page (or the missing recording) does not settle the answer.
- **Open** — the activity is personal or creative; the reveal shows an illustrative example that is explicitly not a key.
- **Picture inference** — the answer follows only from the arrangement of the printed pictures and options, not from the printed words.

## 3. Workbook p. 28, exercise 1 — the five picture numbers

The printed panel is `1 Vaccination · 2 Television · 3 Wi-Fi · 4 Radium · 5 Electric light`. The five printed pictures show a light bulb, a television set, a hand holding a Wi-Fi icon, a syringe with a vial, and a **radiograph of a hand wearing a ring**.

| Number | Picture | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | light bulb | Printed word *Electric light* |
| 2 | television set | Printed word *Television* |
| 3 | hand with a Wi-Fi icon | Printed word *Wi-Fi* |
| 1 | syringe and vial | Printed word *Vaccination* |
| 4 | radiograph of a hand | **Picture inference** — the last picture left over, paired with the last word left over |

The five numbers are fixed because they are the only way to use up the printed panel exactly once. That is a **grouping device of the exercise**, and the page says so in those words.

Two claims are explicitly **not** made anywhere on the lesson pages:

1. that the picture *is* radium;
2. that radium and a radiograph are the same thing.

The workbook page states the opposite in as many words: *“that pairing is the book’s grouping device and is not a statement that an X-ray picture is radium, or that radium and a radiograph are the same thing. Radium is a chemical element; a radiograph is a photographic image made with X-rays.”* The reveal repeats it, and `tests/grade5-unit1-lesson8.mjs` and `tests/grade5-unit1-lesson8.browser.mjs` both assert that the disclaimer is present.

## 6. What the lesson does not do

- It does not add a date, a nationality or an achievement that the six supplied pages do not print.
- It does not reorder exercises, renumber them, or merge two printed activities into one.
- It does not present any of the open prompts as having a correct answer.
- It does not present the radiograph as radium.

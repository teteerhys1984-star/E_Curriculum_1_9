# Source-by-source review — Grade 5, Unit 1, Lesson 3

## Reference and method

- The title printed on both books is **3 Daily Routines** (Pupil’s Book) and **3 Daily Routine** (Workbook).
- The six supplied images are the authoritative source. No OCR text was used in place of reading them.
- Images 1–3 are Pupil’s Book pages **9, 10 and 11**. Images 4–6 are Workbook pages **11, 12 and 13**. The two books are kept separate and in their original page order.
- The six uploads are preserved unchanged as `assets/grade5-unit1-lesson3/source/*.png`. Their SHA-256 hashes, sizes and dimensions are recorded in the [source manifest](../../assets/grade5-unit1-lesson3/source-manifest.json) and re-checked on every `npm test` run.
- Fourteen crops were cut from those pages for the pictures and clocks that the pages need to stay complete; their hashes are recorded in the [crops manifest](../../assets/grade5-unit1-lesson3/crops-manifest.json) and are re-checked too. Fifteen crop files exist; all are referenced.
- Nothing is re-typed from imagination. Everything the images do not settle is listed under “Uncertain or incomplete” below and is also marked on the page where it appears.
- Arabic appears only in the Vocabulary page, inside `.ar lang="ar" dir="rtl"` cells, and only as the meaning of an English word printed in the lesson.

## Uncertain or incomplete — recorded, not guessed

1. **The eight printed numbers in Workbook exercise 6a.** The passage carries eight underlined mistakes, but the numbers printed beside them run (1) (2) (3) (4) (5) (7) (8). **No number (6) is printed anywhere in the passage**, and the margin answer box is numbered 1–8. The page keeps the numbering exactly as printed: seven fields numbered 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 7 and 8, and a written note explaining that the sixth box has no printed marker. No sixth mistake was invented.
2. **The blank clock labels in Workbook exercise 3.** The twelve blue labels around the dial are **empty in the printed book** — nothing is printed inside any of them. The labels are also placed as brush strokes rather than on exact hour marks. The page therefore keeps twelve blank labels and reproduces the twelve-item word bank, states that the hands show **twenty-five to five**, and does **not** publish a label-by-label key.
3. **Pupil’s Book activity 1 and activity 2, and Workbook exercise 4 — listening.** The recordings were not supplied. Every printed instruction, picture, pair and option is kept, each item can be answered and the answer is stored, but no correct option is claimed and these items are not scored. No transcript and no audio file has been invented.
4. **Pupil’s Book activity 6a items 1 and 5.** The passage says “I love Friday. It’s a holiday.” and “We never go to bed early on Thursdays”. Neither “weekend” nor “Saturday” appears in the text, so *Mira likes weekends* and *Mira goes to bed early on Saturdays* cannot be settled with certainty from the printed passage. Both are left answerable and the revealed note says they are not settled by the text rather than giving an invented T/F.
5. **Pupil’s Book activity 3.** Either printed phrase completes either question, and the book ties neither to one question. Both selections are accepted and nothing is marked wrong.
6. **Pupil’s Book activity 4.** Each printed form ends in a dotted gap with no hour printed, so the gap accepts the hour the learner counts from or to. The page states what the gap is for and gives one worked example from the printed speech bubble rather than a full key.
7. **Workbook exercise 6b.** The three column headings are printed, with one example item in each. The completed table is not printed in the book, so the answer shown is described as the routine the text gives, and any wording the pupil uses is accepted.
8. **Spellings kept as printed.** Pupil’s Book page 10 prints **`grage`** and **`houswork`**; Workbook page 13 prints **`grage`** as well. All are reproduced unchanged and named in a note instead of being silently corrected.

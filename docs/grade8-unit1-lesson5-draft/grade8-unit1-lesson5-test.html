Teacher guidance and answer key

The classroom password hides the published key on screen; it is not secure authentication because the key is included in the page source.

  1. humiliate them — Student’s Book p. 36 matches humiliate with embarrass. Retain means keep; evaluate means judge.
  2. grief — Workbook p. 18 defines grief as sadness; dignity and support mean different things.
  3. judge it — Workbook p. 18 defines evaluate as judging how good something is, not ignoring or repeating it.
  4. a trusted teacher — Student’s Book p. 35 names a teacher as someone to talk to; a smartphone and a video game are not people.
  5. True — Student’s Book p. 35 says cyber bullying does not require face-to-face contact.
  6. False — Workbook p. 18 explains that an online image does not always reflect real feelings.
  7. would take — The second conditional uses would plus a base verb in the result clause.
  8. managed — The second conditional uses a simple past form in the if-clause.
  9. If I were you, I would talk to a teacher. — The if-clause uses were and the result uses would plus the base verb.
  10. She talked with a friend. — The new passage says Lina talked with her friend; it mentions neither posting nor buying a phone.
  11. lonely — The new passage says Lina felt lonely, not excited or angry.
  12. Why don’t you talk to a trusted adult? — Why don’t you plus a base verb makes a suggestion; the other choices are statements.

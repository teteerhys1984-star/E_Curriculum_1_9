The six supplied PNGs are the only authoritative textbook source. Images 1–3 are the **Pupil’s Book**, printed pages **9, 10, 11**; images 4–6 are the **Workbook**, also printed pages **9, 10, 11**. Both books title this lesson **My Lovely School** and show a circled **3** lesson badge. The requested project location is Grade 3, **Unit 1**, Lesson 3; the printed badge is not changed.

Every exercise, dialogue, sentence, option, visible label, number and response space was checked visually, including enlarged crops of the small pictures. Line breaks and decorative spacing may reflow on screen; wording, punctuation, item order and the distinction between printed examples and blank responses are retained. Curly/straight apostrophes are typographic variants, not different wording. Supporting instructions, Arabic meanings and answer explanations are explicitly editorial, never presented as textbook quotations.

No exercise text is unreadable. Tiny decorative marks inside drawings are not invented words or numbers; the original illustrations remain available. No audio files were supplied. In particular, the six Workbook listening numbers cannot be inferred from the pictures.

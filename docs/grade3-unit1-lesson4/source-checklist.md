Lesson: **My Five Senses** · Pupil’s Book pages **12–14**, Workbook pages **12–14** · supplied images **1–6**.

The six supplied PNGs are the only authoritative textbook source. Images 1–3 are the **Pupil’s Book**, printed pages **12, 13, 14**; images 4–6 are the **Workbook**, also printed pages **12, 13, 14**. Both books title the lesson **My Five Senses** and show the circled lesson badge on every page. This project places the lesson at Grade 3, Unit 1, Lesson 4; the printed badge is not changed.

Every original scan is retained byte-for-byte with a checksum manifest, and each activity is shown as a direct crop of that scan (27 crops) — never a redrawn or generated illustration. Pupil’s Book and Workbook stay on separate pages, in printed order. No textbook answer is placed in a student field before the student acts, and no listening answer is published.

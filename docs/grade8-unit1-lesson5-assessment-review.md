# Grade 8 Lesson 5 — candidate assessment review

The assessment is editorial and independent, not a transcription of a textbook exercise. Source references identify the taught language or objective, not a claimed printed answer key. Q4 was revised to ask about a person explicitly named in the reading, with clearly non-human distractors. The key is hidden by a classroom password in the draft UI, but the client-side key and password are not secure authentication.

| No. | Source objective | Answer | Explanation and distractors |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Student's Book p. 36 word/definition match | humiliate them | The printed matching pairs humiliate with embarrass. Retain means keep; evaluate means judge. |
| 2 | Workbook p. 18 Key Words | grief | The Key Words box defines grief as sadness. Dignity is a sense of one's worth and support is help. |
| 3 | Workbook p. 18 Key Words | judge it | Evaluate means judge how good something is. Ignore and repeat have different meanings. |
| 4 | Student's Book p. 35, How to deal with a bully? | a trusted teacher | The reading names a teacher as someone to talk to; a smartphone and a video game are not people. |
| 5 | Student's Book p. 35, What is bullying? | True | The reading says cyber bullying does not require face-to-face contact; false contradicts it. |
| 6 | Workbook p. 18, social media reading | False | The text says online appearances do not always reflect real feelings; true incorrectly makes this universal. |
| 7 | Workbook p. 20, second conditional result clause | would take | A hypothetical past if-clause takes would plus base verb in the result; takes and will take do not follow the taught second-conditional form. |
| 8 | Workbook p. 20, second conditional if-clause | managed | The taught if-clause uses simple past; manage and will manage are not that form. |
| 9 | Workbook p. 20, were and would + base form | If I were you, I would talk to a teacher. | This uses were in the if-clause and would plus base form. The first distractor uses would in the if-clause and lacks would in the result; the second uses am and would talked. |
| 10 | Workbook p. 18, understanding online feelings and taking a break | She talked with a friend. | This follows the new passage directly; the passage does not say she posted another photo or bought a phone. |
| 11 | Workbook p. 18, feelings and loneliness | lonely | The new passage says Lina felt lonely; it does not say she felt excited or angry. |
| 12 | Workbook p. 21, making suggestions | Why don’t you talk to a trusted adult? | The printed suggestion frame is “Why don't you” plus infinitive. The other sentences describe past or present facts, not suggestions. |

The twelve answers above were solved from the taught objectives and the two original reading questions, then compared with the displayed options and gated key. The browser suite checks the separately listed answers against the selections and key, scores 12/12 and 0/12, and verifies reset and persistence. No listening or pronunciation item is included in the score.

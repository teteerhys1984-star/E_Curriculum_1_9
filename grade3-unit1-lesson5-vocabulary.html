7. Language focus

Talking about your school

Upstairs and downstairs name the floor; on the right of names the position. These sentences are quoted from the revision page:

The classrooms are downstairs.

The library is on the right of the laboratory.

The laboratory is upstairs.

Talking about subjects

Use I’ve got + subject + and + subject, plus on + day, and My favourite subject is …

I've got Science and Music on Sunday.

We've got English and Maths on Monday.

My favourite subject is Art.

Talking about people at home

She's got two brothers.

I live with my parents in a flat.

The revision sounds

The revision asks pupils to hear and write four letter pairs: oo, ou, ea and oa. The printed words include broom, house, meat and goat. The original recordings were not supplied, so no listening answer is published here.

📖 Exercise 6 · Read and do the tasks

Hello my friends,

I’m Ahmed. I’m ten years old and I’m in grade 4. I’m from Lebanon, but I live in Syria. My father is a doctor and my mother is a teacher. I’ve got two little brothers, Adam and Allan. They are twins. We are all in the same school. It is a very big school.

a. Tick ✓ or cross ×

1. Ahmed is from Syria.

2. Adam and Allan are in Ahmed’s class.

The passage confirms they attend the same school, not whether they are in the same class.

3. Ahmed’s mother is a teacher.

1. False. 2. Not confirmed by the passage. 3. True.

b. Fill in with am / is / are

Hello, I Ahmed. I from Lebanon. My father a doctor. Adam and Allan my little brothers. Our school very big.

am · am · is · are · is

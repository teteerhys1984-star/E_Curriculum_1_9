5. Grammar notes in English

  • is + adjective describes the whole person: He is tall. She is short. He is tall and thin.
  • has got + hair or eyes describes a part of the person: He’s got blond hair. She’s got brown eyes.
  • I and they take have got; he, she and it take has got.
  • The question form printed in the lesson is What does he / she look like?

These notes are teaching support written for this page. They use only the sentences and forms printed in the supplied pages.

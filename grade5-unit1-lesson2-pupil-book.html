To ask about the number of siblings, use “How many brothers and sisters have you got?” A possible answer is “I’ve got one brother.”

Sample dialogue:

B: I come from a small family.
A: How many brothers and sisters have you got?

Page 8 · Let’s Learn: Possessives

He is Lara’s uncle.
She is Charles’s mother.
Those are the children’s toys.
These are the students’ books.

We usually add ’s to a singular noun to show possession or a relationship: “Lara’s uncle.” With regular plurals ending in s, put the apostrophe after the s: “students’ books.” Children is an irregular plural, so write “children’s toys.”

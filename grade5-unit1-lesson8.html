GRADE 5 · UNIT 1 · LESSON 8

Inventions

Talk about inventions and inventors, name the discoveries they led to, use the past simple to describe what inventors did, read about three inventions that changed the world, and write about a favourite invention. Source material is separated by book and kept in original page order.

Pupil’s Book

Pages 27–29 · invention words, the inventor panel, pronunciation u:, and the past simple.

Workbook

Pages 28–30 · matching inventions, the inventor boxes, a word search and a writing task.

Vocabulary & Explanation

Inventions, inventors and the past simple, with Arabic meanings.

Lesson Test

18 independent questions and a separate teacher-answer-key area.

Learning goals

  • Name inventions, inventors and discoveries.
  • Read and understand a short text about three inventions.
  • Use the past simple to describe what inventors did.
  • Say what a person did with did questions and short answers.
  • Write sentences about what an inventor did.
  • Write a short paragraph about a favourite invention.

Every exercise on the supplied book pages can be answered on screen. Where the book prints an answer, that answer is shown and marked as printed. Where the book prints no key, the exercise stores your answer and shows a suggested answer only where the page itself settles it. Listening tasks keep every printed instruction, picture and option, but no answer is invented and no audio file has been created.

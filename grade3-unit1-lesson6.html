Learning goals

  • Say how often you do a routine action, using always, usually, often, sometimes and never.
  • Add s to the verb when the subject is he, she or a name, and put never before the main verb.
  • Ask and answer “What do you do every day?” and talk about your own routine.
  • Recognise the sound and spelling gr in grass and grapes.

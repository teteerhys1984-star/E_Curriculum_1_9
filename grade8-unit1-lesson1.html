GRADE 8 · UNIT 1 · LESSON 1

Future Plans

Learn to talk about schools in the past, present and future, use the lesson’s key words, and revise the present simple, present progressive and future tenses. The Student’s Book and the Workbook are two separate book strands and are kept apart here, in their original page order.

📘 Student’s Book

Pages 6–11 · unit opener, reading “Schools in the Past, Present and Future”, key words, tense revision, listening and writing.

✏️ Workbook

Pages 2–5 · reading “School Life”, vocabulary matching, grammar reference tables, listening jokes and writing practice.

🔤 Vocabulary & English Grammar Notes

English vocabulary with word-level Arabic meanings and grammar notes in English.

📝 Lesson Test

16 questions and a separate teacher answer-key area. Listening is not scored because no recording is available.

Learning goals

  • Talk about schools in the past, present and future.
  • Understand two reading texts: “Schools in the Past, Present and Future” (Student’s Book) and “School Life” (Workbook).
  • Use the printed key words: provide, innovations, major, rely, concept, combine, convey, practical, flexible, personalize, format — and the Workbook set: concept, independently, attitude, accustomed to, familiarity, expose, remarkable.
  • Revise the present simple, present progressive and future tenses (will / going to).
  • Complete a guided paragraph about “my future school”.

Listening activities are retained as printed, but the required recordings are unavailable. No answers have been invented, and listening is excluded from automatic test scoring.

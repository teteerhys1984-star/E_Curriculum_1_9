How Each Lesson Is Organized

Consistent four-part structure across primary and intermediate grades so students and teachers always know where to find source material and practice.

1. Pupil’s / Student’s Book

Page-referenced dialogues, reading texts, grammar tables, and step-by-step classroom activities faithful to the printed book.

Core study

2. Workbook

Interactive matching, sentence completion, grammar drills, and self-check answer guidance clearly separated from Student’s Book pages.

Guided practice

3. Vocabulary

Clean, structured word and phrase tables organized by topic, with accurate word-level Arabic meanings isolated cleanly for bilingual study.

Word reference

4. Lesson Test

Every lesson test is built strictly from verified book evidence, navigated one question at a time, and scored instantly on its objective items. Published tests hold between 10 and 20 questions.

20-item check

For Teachers

A single entry point to the published lessons, the answer-key areas, and an honest list of the material that does not exist yet.

No sign-in

Teacher Portal

The teacher portal collects the seven prepared lessons, the answer-key areas published on six lesson tests, and the practice answer reveals that pupils can already open. It also states plainly what is not available: no audio key, no round test, no teacher’s guide, and no accounts or pupil records.

The classroom password on the answer-key areas only hides those answers from casual view. It is not authentication and does not protect confidential material.

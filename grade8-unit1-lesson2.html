GRADE 8 · UNIT 1 · LESSON 2

School Challenges

Returning to school, worries about school life, bullying, and how to deal with them. The Student’s Book and the Workbook are two separate book strands and are kept apart here, in their original page order.

Student’s Book

Printed pages 12–18 · unit opener and speaking survey, reading “A Worrying Time”, key words, tense revision, listening, pronunciation, writing, a physics focus and Project 1.

Workbook

Printed pages 6–9 · speaking and reading “Bullying”, true/false and multiple-choice tasks, verb–noun table, past simple and past progressive grammar, listening and writing.

Vocabulary & English Grammar Notes

English vocabulary with word-level Arabic meanings and grammar notes in English.

Lesson Test

12 questions and a separate teacher answer-key area. Listening and writing are not scored because no recording is available.

Learning goals

  • Talk about returning to school, school worries and how to overcome difficulties.
  • Understand two reading texts: “A Worrying Time” (Student’s Book) and “Bullying” (Workbook).
  • Use the key vocabulary and expressions from both books (for example expose, depressed, self-esteem, hesitate, bully, complicated, on purpose, belongings, oblige, jealous).
  • Revise verb tenses: present simple, present progressive, future (will / going to), past simple and past progressive.
  • Recognise silent letters ⟨h⟩ and ⟨gh⟩ and join sentences with the conjunctions and, but and or.

Listening and writing activities are retained as printed, but the required recordings are unavailable. No answers have been invented, and listening and free writing are excluded from automatic test scoring.

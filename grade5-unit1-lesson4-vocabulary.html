Grammar notes

1. Sense verbs + adjective

After look, sound, feel, smell and taste we usually put an adjective, not an adverb: The music sounds loud. · The cat feels soft. · Flowers smell nice.

2. Third-person -s

With he, she or it the present simple verb takes -s: The cat feels soft. · Hot chocolate tastes sweet. With I, you, we and they there is no -s: Flowers smell nice.

3. Questions with do and does

Do + I / you / we / they + plain verb: Do you smell my perfume? · Does + he / she / it + plain verb: How does the jacket feel? After does the verb has no -s.

4. Negative sentences

don’t / doesn’t + plain verb: Lemons don’t taste salty. · Salma doesn’t like loud music.

5. Wh-questions

How does wood feel? → It feels hard. · What sound do you hear? → I hear a car. The question word comes first, then do or does, then the subject and the plain verb.

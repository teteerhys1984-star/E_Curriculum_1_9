Vocabulary · Interesting Destinations

Two kinds of vocabulary, kept apart: the items the books list with meanings (Student’s Book p. 27 and Workbook p. 15, plus the three matching words on Student’s Book p. 31), and additional useful words and expressions gathered by reading every one of the eleven supplied pages. Every row has an Arabic meaning, and every entry is traceable to a printed page.

GRADE 5 · UNIT 1 · LESSON 3

Daily Routines

Talk about daily routines, tell the time, and use frequency adverbs with the present simple. Source material is separated by book and kept in original page order.

This lesson practises six routine verbs (get up, have a shower, pack my bag, set the table, chat online, go to bed), the frequency adverbs always, usually, sometimes and never, telling the time with past, to, quarter and half, and the -s / -es forms of the present simple with the questions Do …? and Does …?.

Pupil’s Book

Pages 9–11 · listening and circling, asking and answering, the clock, a chart, reading, and grammar practice.

Workbook

Pages 11–13 · matching, reordering, telling the time, listening, a chart, reading and writing.

Vocabulary & Explanation

Routine verbs, time expressions and frequency adverbs, with Arabic meanings and the grammar rules the pages practise.

Lesson Test

18 independent questions and a separate teacher answer-key area.

Learning goals

  • Name the everyday actions of a daily routine.
  • Say how often something happens with always, usually, sometimes and never.
  • Read a simple chart and turn it into sentences.
  • Tell the time with o’clock, past, to, quarter, half and twenty-five.
  • Use the present simple correctly: he / she / it takes -s, and questions use Do or Does.
  • Read a short routine text and answer true / false and who-does-what questions.

Every exercise on the three Pupil’s Book pages and the three Workbook pages can be answered on screen. Where the book prints an answer, that answer is shown and marked as printed. Where the book prints no key, the exercise stores your answer and shows a suggested answer only where the page itself settles it.

Two listening exercises need the audio, which was not supplied. Pupil’s Book activity 1, Pupil’s Book activity 2 and Workbook exercise 4 are listening tasks. The printed instructions, options and pictures are all kept, and you can record your own answer, but no correct answer is invented and these items are not scored. No audio file has been created.

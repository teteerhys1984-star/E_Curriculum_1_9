Lesson overview

Explore forms of transport and journeys; discuss a trip to Spain and a trip to Australia; practise present perfect and reported speech; listen when the official recordings are available; and write about a journey.

The repository route is Grade 8 · Unit 1 · Lesson 3. The supplied textbook pages themselves are headed Module 2 · Unit 3; the Workbook adds Journeys Around the World. Both labels are retained rather than changing the site route.

Student’s Book pp. 20–25 and Workbook pp. 10–13 are kept separate. Original images are linked from their corresponding pages. Listening is unscored until the official recordings are available.

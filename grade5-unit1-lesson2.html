GRADE 5 · UNIT 1 · LESSON 2

Family

Learn to name family members, describe relatives, use possessive forms and practise plural nouns. Source material is separated by book and original page order.

This lesson introduces names for family members, ways to talk about relatives, possession with ’s and apostrophes after plural nouns, and plural forms.

Pupil’s Book

Pages 6–8 · reading, dialogues, family relationships, possessives and activities.

Workbook

Pages 8–10 · reading, family tree, plurals, possessions and writing practice.

Vocabulary & Explanation

English vocabulary with Arabic meanings and detailed grammar explanations.

Lesson Test

20 questions and a separate teacher answer-key area.

Learning goals

  • Name and identify family members.
  • Describe relatives and talk about family size.
  • Understand a family description.
  • Use possessive ’s and plural possessive apostrophe.
  • Recognize irregular plurals: man/men, woman/women, child/children.

We will not invent answers for listening activities that require unavailable recordings or decide unclear diagram details without checking the source.

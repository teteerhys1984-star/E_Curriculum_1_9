My Uncle’s Shop

My uncle has a small clothes shop by the sea. Last Saturday, I helped him all morning. First, I put the plain T-shirts on a table. Then I put three dresses on the clothes line: a dotted blue dress, a floral red dress and a striped green dress. A woman came in. She said, “I am looking for a coat.” My uncle showed her a black coat and a blue jacket. The woman took the black coat. It cost 4000 SP. In the afternoon, we had our lunch in the shade. Next Saturday, I am helping him again.

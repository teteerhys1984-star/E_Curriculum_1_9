Grammar notes

1. be going to for plans

Use am / is / are + going to + the plain verb to talk about a plan: I am going to play the guitar. · Lily is going to sing. · They are going to play music.

2. Choosing am, is or are

I am … · he / she / it / Lily is … · you / we / they / the students are …. The subject decides the form, never the time word.

3. Negative: isn’t / aren’t going to

Put not after the be verb: She is not going to go to the concert. · She isn’t going to dance in the concert. · They aren’t going to play tonight.

4. Questions with be going to

Put the be verb first: Are you going to play the tambourine? · What are you going to do in the concert? Short answers repeat the be verb: Yes, I am. / No, I’m not.

5. Time words for the future

tonight, tomorrow, next week and in the evening all look forward. Time words such as yesterday and last week look back, so they do not fit a going to sentence.

6. Two vowels: æ and ɑː

The short æ is the vowel in cat, mat and bat. The long ɑː is the vowel in car, far and jar. The two sound different: cat is not cart, and mat is not mart. Model sentence: The fat cat sat on the mat.

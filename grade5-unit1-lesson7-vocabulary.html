Grammar notes

1. can + the plain verb

can is followed by the plain verb, never by to or -ing: I can read. · She can make pizza. · She can listen to music.

2. can’t for things you cannot do

can’t (cannot) says that something is not possible: I can’t read difficult books. · He is too young. He can’t drive a car.

3. Questions with Can …?

Put can before the person: Can you ride a bike? Short answers repeat it: Yes, I can. / No, I can’t.

4. Telling time and frequency

Use in my free time, every week and at the weekends to say when: I can read in my free time. · I go to the library every week. · She cooks at the weekends.

5. Hobby + -ing

Hobbies are often named with an -ing word: reading, drawing, painting, dancing, cooking. Some hobby phrases keep their own verb: take photographs, play chess, do karate.

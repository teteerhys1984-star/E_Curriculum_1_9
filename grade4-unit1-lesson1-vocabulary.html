WORD GARDEN 🌱

Vocabulary Studio

Use these words to greet people, introduce yourself, talk about where you live, and describe your family. Read each example aloud, then try the mini-check.

👋 Greetings🧑 About me🌍 Countries👨‍👩‍👧 Family
👋

Hello / Hi

Words we use when we meet someone.

“Hello, Jane!”

🤝

Nice to meet you

A friendly phrase for meeting someone for the first time.

“Nice to meet you, Ahmed.”

🪪

Name

What someone is called.

“My name is Adam.”

🎂

Age

How old a person is.

“I am ten.”

🌍

From

Use it to say which place or country a person comes from.

“I am from Lebanon.”

🏡

Live

To have your home in a place.

“I live in Syria.”

👨‍👩‍👧

Family

The people in your family.

“This is my family.”

👬

Twin brothers

Two brothers born at the same time.

“They are twin brothers.”

🩺

Doctor

A person whose job is to help sick people.

“My father is a doctor.”

📚

Teacher

A person whose job is to teach.

“My mother is a teacher.”

🔢

Numbers

Practise number words from one to twenty.

one · two · three · … · twenty

✨

am / is / are

Forms of the verb be used to talk about people and things.

I am · He is · They are

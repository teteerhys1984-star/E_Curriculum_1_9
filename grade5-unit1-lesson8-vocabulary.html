Grammar notes

1. Past simple for finished actions

The past simple talks about something that is finished: Bell invented the telephone. · She developed a technology. · They changed our world.

2. Regular verbs add -ed

invent → invented · discover → discovered · improve → improved. A verb ending in a consonant + y changes the y to i: carry → carried · try → tried.

3. Irregular verbs change inside the word

make → made · see → saw · are → were · become → became · have → had · fly → flew. These forms are not made with -ed.

4. Negative and questions with did

With did the verb goes back to its base form: She didn’t invent the telephone. · Did Bell invent the telephone? — Yes, he did. Not Did Bell invented…

5. inventor, invent, invention

The person is the inventor, the action is invent, and the thing is the invention: An inventor is a person who invents things; the things are inventions. The plural is inventions.

6. Talking about the past and today

Use In the past … and Today … to compare: In the past, life was different from today. · Today, we use Wi-Fi every day.

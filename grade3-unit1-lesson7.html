Learning goals

  • Tell the time to the quarter hour and the half hour, using quarter past, quarter to and half past.
  • Ask and answer “What time does it start?” about TV programmes, and use starts or start with the right subject.
  • Use at with clock times and on with days, and write sentences that start with a capital letter and end with a full stop.
  • Recognise the sound and spelling sl in sleep and slide.

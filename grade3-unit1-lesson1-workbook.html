2. Where do you live?

3. Who do you live with?

4. What’s your brother’s name?

Listen and circle

EXERCISE 4

Read and write

Complete with have got or has got.

Maya _____ a little sister.

I _____ two brothers.

He _____ a car.

We _____ a nice flat.

Maya has got a little sister. · I have got two brothers. · He has got a car. · We have got a nice flat.

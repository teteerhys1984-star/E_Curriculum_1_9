03 · Grammar made simple

Have got and has got

Use “have got”

I have got a book.
You have got a pencil.
We have got a big house.
They have got two brothers.

Short form: I’ve got, you’ve got, we’ve got, they’ve got.

Use “has got”

He has got a ball.
She has got a sister.
It has got four legs.

Short form: he’s got, she’s got, it’s got. In this lesson, “he’s got” means “he has got”.

Easy way to remember

I / you / we / they → have got
he / she / it → has got

Common mistake: don’t say “He have got”. Say “He has got”.

Talking about your family

I live with my family. = أنا أعيش مع عائلتي.
I’ve got two brothers. = لديّ أخوان.
Who do you live with? = مع من تعيش؟

Exercise 4 · Read and write

Complete each sentence with have got or has got.

1. Maya ______ a little sister.
2. I ______ two brothers.
3. He ______ a car.
4. We ______ a nice flat.

Answers with reasons:
1. Maya has got a little sister. — Maya = she.
2. I have got two brothers. — use have got with I.
3. He has got a car. — use has got with he.
4. We have got a nice flat. — use have got with we.

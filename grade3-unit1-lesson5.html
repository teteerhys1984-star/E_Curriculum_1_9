Learning goals

  • Read where school rooms are and say whether a room is upstairs or downstairs, left or right.
  • Name school subjects, say which subjects you have got on a day, and talk about a favourite subject.
  • Read the tens numbers up to fifty and continue a simple number pattern.
  • Talk about brothers, sisters and where a family lives.
  • Recognise and write the revision letter pairs oo, ou, ea and oa in known words.

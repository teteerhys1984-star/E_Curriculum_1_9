Question 13 of 18 · Reading

13. Read the story. Then choose True or False: Kareem used his eyes in the game.

Kareem’s Four Boxes

On Friday, our class played a game with four boxes. My friend Kareem could not see, so he used his other senses. In box one, he felt a cold glass and some soft cotton. In box two, he smelled a flower and a lemon. The flower smelled nice, but the lemon smelled strong. In box three, he tasted a piece of cake and a piece of chocolate. The cake was sweet, but the chocolate was bitter. In box four, he heard loud music and quiet music. He liked the quiet music best. Kareem was right every time. We can know many things with our ears, our nose, our tongue and our skin.

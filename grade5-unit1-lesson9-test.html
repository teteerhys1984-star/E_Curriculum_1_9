13. Read the text. Which instrument does Nour play?

Nour’s Concert

A short original passage written only for this assessment.

Nour is twelve years old and she lives in Homs. She plays the oud in a band with three friends, and her friend Sami plays the tabla. The band is going to play in a concert at their school next Friday. Nour is excited, but she is a little worried too, because her family is going to come and listen. She practises every evening after her homework. On Monday she practised for two hours and her fingers hurt, but she did not stop. “Do not worry,” says Sami. “We are going to play well.” Nour is going to sing one song as well, and she wrote the words herself. She is not going to play a long solo. She wants the band to sound together, like one voice.

EXERCISE 4

Practise with a partner · School rules

Don’t run in the corridor; you have to walk.

have to expresses obligation. don’t have to means something is not necessary; it does not mean “must not”.

لا تركض في الممر؛ يجب أن تمشي. “have to” = يجب أن، أما “don’t have to” = ليس من الضروري أن.

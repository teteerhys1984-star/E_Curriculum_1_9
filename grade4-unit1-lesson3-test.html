Original test stimulus

Farid’s Saturday

On Saturday morning, Farid gets up at half past seven and makes his bed. His sister gets up at eight. They brush their teeth, then read a short story at home. Before lunch, Farid washes his face and helps Mum with breakfast. In the afternoon, they play in the garden. At half past eight, Farid goes to bed.

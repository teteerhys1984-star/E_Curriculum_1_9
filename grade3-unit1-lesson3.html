Learning goals

  • Name school and house places and sort them into the correct category.
  • Ask and answer about location using upstairs, downstairs, left, right and between.
  • Use there is / there are and How many with the lesson’s quantities.
  • Describe abilities and preferences with can, prefer and favourite.
  • Ask for help, give a location and respond politely; recognise ea in meat and eat.

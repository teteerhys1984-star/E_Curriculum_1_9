Page 13 · Reading: A Worrying Time

Read the text and do the tasks below. (The tasks on page 13 continue below, and the vocabulary and grammar tasks follow on page 14.)

What about being back to school? Does thinking of being back to school worry you? Schools are good places for socializing, but everyone worries about things that happen at school from time to time. No single person passed through school without experiencing some problems or events.

The stresses of school life that we are exposed to make us sometimes feel unhappy, depressed, out of control and have low self-esteem. We may feel less motivated in our classrooms or careless about doing homework so that our marks may become low and we don't get good results. Sometimes we find it hard to get up and get ready for school.

Some people may hesitate to go to school, or even refuse to go at all. School problems we may worry about are, for example, finding school work difficult, having problems in classrooms especially if others are noisy, not getting on with teachers, problems at home, and bullies who often cause complicated problems, etc.

In this situation, the most important thing is to recognize that there's a problem to be solved and ask for help as soon as possible. We may ask our parents at home, or teachers and social workers in schools. They can help in solving the problems that we face.

Page 14 · Grammar in context: Revision of Tenses

Complete the following paragraph with the suitable verb tenses from the list

was carrying, was wondering, came, was walking, decided, came up, stood

I won't forget what had happened before I(1)…………… to live here. I'm going to tell you the story which happened ten years ago.I(2)…………… down the street when suddenly a man(3)…………… to me and asked me to lend him some money. Well, he(4)…………… a big stick and he looked a bit dangerous. I(5)…………… still in my place for about five seconds and I(6)…………… what to do. Then I(7)…………… to run away without the slightest hesitation.

The paragraph and the verb list are reproduced exactly as printed, including the missing space in “ten years ago.I”. Seven printed blanks are answerable.

Select the correct tense between brackets.

  1. My stereo (stops - stopped) working last night.
    stopped — “last night” signals a finished past action: past simple.
  2. The weather (is - was) dreadful at the weekend.
    was — “at the weekend” places the description in the past.
  3. Samer (was taking - is taking) a shower when the telephone rang.
    was taking — an action in progress when a shorter past action happened.
  4. John was playing tennis when he (hurts - hurt) his leg.
    hurt — the shorter interrupting action is past simple.
  5. I (was going - went) home when I met my friend.
    went — “when I met my friend” marks a past event; “went home” is the past-simple action.

Page 17 · Focus on Physics

1- Do you love physics? Why?

2- How do we use physics in our daily life?

Newton's laws of motion relate an object's motion to the forces acting on it.

The 1st law: An object will not change its motion unless a force acts on it.

The 2nd law: The acceleration of an object depends on two things, force and mass. (force = mass x acceleration)

The 3rd law: For every action, there is an equal and opposite reaction.

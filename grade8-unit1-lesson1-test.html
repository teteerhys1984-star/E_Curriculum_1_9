LESSON CHECK

Check your understanding

A short independent check on reading, vocabulary and tense choice. Listening is excluded from scoring because no recording is available.

Part A · Reading and vocabulary

1. What helped change schools in the 21st century?
2. Modern technologies have played a ___ role in education.
3. To “rely on” something means to…
4. A future school combines education with…
5. “Independently” most nearly means…
6. Which is a present progressive sentence?
7. Choose the present simple form: Hani ___ ice-cream whenever we go to the park.
8. Choose the future form: They ___ the museum next week.

Part B · Writing

Write 5–7 sentences about how you imagine a future school. Include one present simple sentence, one present progressive sentence, and one future sentence.

Self-check: capital letters, full stops, clear ideas, and correct tense forms.

GRADE 5 · UNIT 1 · LESSON 6

Healthy Lifestyle

Talk about a healthy lifestyle, name the food groups, give advice with should and shouldn’t, suggest with Why don’t you …?, read about healthy eating, and write about your own habits. Source material is separated by book and kept in original page order.

Pupil’s Book

Pages 21–23 · listening, food groups, the “My Plate” text, and advice with should and shouldn’t.

Workbook

Pages 22–24 · healthy habits, a question game, a doctor dialogue, and a reading about the Thomas family.

Vocabulary & Explanation

Food groups, healthy habits, should / shouldn’t and Why don’t you …?, with Arabic meanings.

Lesson Test

18 independent questions and a separate teacher-answer-key area.

Learning goals

  • Name food and habits that are good or bad for your health.
  • Name the four food groups: dairy, meat and fish, grains, and fruit and vegetables.
  • Give advice with should and shouldn’t.
  • Make a suggestion with Why don’t you …?
  • Read a short text about healthy eating and answer questions about it.
  • Write sentences of advice and record what you eat each day.

Every exercise on the supplied book pages can be answered on screen. Where the book prints an answer, that answer is shown and marked as printed. Where the book prints no key, the exercise stores your answer and shows a suggested answer only where the page itself settles it. Listening tasks keep every printed instruction, picture and option, but no answer is invented and no audio file has been created.

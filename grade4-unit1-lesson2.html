My Family

This lesson is about the people in a family and how they look. You will name family members, describe hair and eyes with has got, describe people with is, read two short family texts, and ask What does he / she look like? Choose one section at a time: the two books stay separate and keep their own page order.

What you will practise

  • Name family members: father, mother, sister, brother, grandfather, grandmother.
  • Describe hair and eyes: brown hair, blond hair, black hair, brown eyes, green eyes, blue eyes.
  • Use is for the whole person and has got for hair and eyes.
  • Ask and answer What does he / she look like?
  • Read a short text about a family and answer questions about it.
  • Read a family table and complete the missing names and relationship words.

This summary is written for this website. It does not replace or change the textbook exercises, and it does not add new learning objectives.

How to use this lesson

  1. Work through the Pupil’s Book page by page, in the order shown.
  2. Then open the Workbook and try each exercise. Checking your own answers is optional; the listening exercises need the class recording.
  3. Use the Vocabulary page as a reference while you write.
  4. Finish with the Lesson Test. Every question names the page it comes from.
Listening note: Pupil’s Book activities 1 and 2 and Workbook exercises 3 and 4 need the official recording. It was not supplied with these pages, so no transcript and no answer has been invented, and none of these items is scored.

GRADE 5 · UNIT 1 · LESSON 5

Clothes

Talk about clothes, describe patterns, use the present continuous, and work with the end-of-unit Revision 1 and Project pages. Source material is separated by book and kept in original page order.

This lesson practises clothes words (pyjamas, gloves, sweater, coat, blouse, skirt), pattern words (floral, striped, dotted, plain), the present continuous (I’m wearing …, She isn’t putting on …), punctuation, and the shopping language Can I help you? and How much is it?

Pupil’s Book

Pages 15–20 · clothes words, patterns, a shopping dialogue, the present continuous, Revision 1 and the Project.

Workbook

Pages 17–21 · reading, clothes writing, listening tasks, -ing forms, Revision 1 and a word search.

Vocabulary & Explanation

Clothes, patterns, shopping phrases and the present continuous, with Arabic meanings and the rules the pages practise.

Lesson Test

18 independent questions and a separate teacher-answer-key area.

Learning goals

  • Name clothes and say what someone is wearing.
  • Describe patterns: floral, striped, dotted and plain.
  • Use the present continuous: am / is / are + verb + -ing, including the negative.
  • Spell -ing forms correctly: swimming, putting, having, taking, buying.
  • Use full stops, commas and question marks correctly.
  • Follow a shopping dialogue and write sentences about clothes.

Every exercise on the supplied book pages can be answered on screen. Where the book prints an answer, that answer is shown and marked as printed. Where the book prints no key, the exercise stores your answer and shows a suggested answer only where the page itself settles it. Listening tasks keep every printed instruction, picture and option, but no answer is invented and no audio file has been created.

Answer Guide & Model Responses

Review these solutions after checking your answers. Written answers may vary; these are sample responses.

  1. have — We use “have got” with I, you, we and they.
  2. has — We use “has got” with he, she and it.
  3. They — “They have got…” is correct.
  4. She — “She has got…” is correct.
  5. عائلة — Family means the people in your family.
  6. أخ — Brother means a male sibling.
  7. الوالدان — Parents means mother and father.
  8. He has got a car. — Use “has got” with he.
  9. have got — “I have got two brothers.”
  10. has got — Maya is one person, so use “has got”.
  11. How old are you? — This asks about age.
  12. Where do you live? — This asks about a place to live.
  13. I live with my parents. — This answers who you live with.
  14. شقة — Flat means apartment.
  15. اليوم — Today means this day.
  16. She has got a little sister. — Other correct family sentences are accepted.
  17. I have got two brothers. — “We have got a big house” is also correct.
  18. How old are you? — This is the question used to ask someone’s age.
  19. Family = عائلة; brother = أخ. — Any two suitable lesson words and correct translations are accepted.
  20. I live in a flat. I live with my parents. — Personal answers will vary; check sentence structure and meaning.

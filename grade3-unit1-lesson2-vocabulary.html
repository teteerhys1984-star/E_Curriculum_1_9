5. English examples from the lesson

These English examples are quoted from the source images. No full sentences are translated into Arabic.

What lessons have we got today?

Today we have got Science and Maths.

What’s your favourite lesson?

My favourite lesson is Maths.

have got and contractions

In this dialogue, have got means that lessons are on the timetable. We’ve got is short for we have got; I’ve got is short for I have got. The negative form haven’t appears in this source dialogue:

Have we got Arabic today?

No, we haven’t. We’ve got Social Studies.

The exchange says that Arabic is not on the timetable today and that Social Studies is instead.

The ou sound

The letters ou represent /aʊ/ in house and mouse. This is an additional pronunciation note. The original recording is unavailable; do not assume that every word containing ou has the same sound.

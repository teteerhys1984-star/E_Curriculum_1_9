Workbook · Family · pages 8–10

Page 8 · 1. Read then do the tasks below.

My mother is 38 years old. She is kind and beautiful. She has got short brown hair. She works as a teacher in a school. She cooks very well. She does all the housework. She often helps me do my homework. She sometimes plays with me. She loves my brother and me very much. I love my mother, she is the best.

a. Choose the correct word

  1. My mother (is / are) 38 years old.
    is
  2. She (have / has) got short hair.
    has
  3. She (work / works) as a teacher.
    works
  4. She can (cook / cooks) very well.
    cook
  5. She loves my brother and (me / my).
    me

b. Cross the odd word out

  1. women / men / children / girl
    girl — the others are plural forms.
  2. niece / nephew / friend / cousin
    friend — the others are family relationships.
  3. mother / son / daughter / granddaughter
    mother — the other three describe a child/descendant relationship. The categorization may depend on the intended lesson grouping.
  4. brother / grandfather / sister / uncle
    This item is ambiguous without the textbook’s intended grouping; ask the teacher or check the original page key.

GRADE 5 · UNIT 1 · LESSON 4

The Five Senses

Talk about the five senses, describe what things look, sound, feel, smell and taste like, and read about how to protect the senses. Source material is separated by book and kept in original page order.

This lesson practises the five senses (sight, touch, smell, taste and hearing), the sense verbs look, sound, feel, smell and taste, describing adjectives, and the questions Do you …? and How does it feel?

Pupil’s Book

Pages 12–14 · listening, looking and describing, reading, sense verbs and writing about the five senses.

Workbook

Pages 14–16 · protecting the senses, matching, do / does, word order and paragraph writing.

Vocabulary & Explanation

Senses, body parts, sense verbs and describing adjectives, with Arabic meanings and the grammar the pages practise.

Lesson Test

18 independent questions and a separate teacher answer-key area.

Learning goals

  • Name the five senses and the body part used for each one.
  • Use look, sound, feel, smell and taste to describe things.
  • Choose adjectives that match a sense: soft / hard, loud / quiet, sweet / bitter / sour / bland.
  • Ask and answer questions with do and does.
  • Read a short text about the senses and answer true / false questions.
  • Write about your own five senses.

Every exercise on the supplied book pages can be answered on screen. Where the book prints an answer, that answer is shown and marked as printed. Where the book prints no key, the exercise stores your answer and shows a suggested answer only where the page itself settles it. Listening tasks keep every printed instruction, picture and option, but no answer is invented and no audio file has been created.

Lesson overview

Talk about famous monuments; read about Iguazu Falls and two travel cities; review the past perfect tense and reported speech; practise the pronunciation of the letter c (/s/ and /k/); listen when the official recordings are available; and complete short writing tasks about a vacation, a climate and an invitation.

The repository route is Grade 8 · Unit 1 · Lesson 4. The supplied textbook pages themselves are headed Unit 4; both labels are kept rather than changing the site route.

Student’s Book pp. 26–32 (images 1–7) and Workbook pp. 14–17 (images 8–11) are kept separate. Every original page image is linked from its corresponding step. Both listening activities are unscored until the official recordings are available; no transcript or listening key has been invented.

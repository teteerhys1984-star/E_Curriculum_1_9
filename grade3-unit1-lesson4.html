Learning goals

  • Name the five senses and the body part used for each one.
  • Use the sense verbs hear, taste, see, smell and touch in the pattern I can … with my ….
  • Read, say and write the tens numbers from ten to fifty, and continue a number sequence up to fifty.
  • Ask and answer with can and can’t, including a polite request with Can I …?
  • Describe things with simple adjectives such as nice, delicious, beautiful, lovely, small and soft; recognise oa in coat and goat.

These are editorial summaries of the supplied lesson, not an extra textbook passage.

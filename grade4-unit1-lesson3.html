By the end, you can…

  • name daily actions and describe a simple routine;
  • say common clock times using o’clock, half past, quarter past and quarter to;
  • choose present-simple verb forms for singular and plural subjects;
  • find stated details in a short routine text and use routine expressions.

These are lesson objectives summarized from the supplied textbook pages; they are not additional book exercises.

Teacher guidance · source limitations
  • Pupil’s Book listening activities 1–2 and Workbook activities 3 and 6 require the original audio, which was not supplied. Their prompts and choices are preserved; no transcripts or answer keys are fabricated.
  • In Pupil’s Book page 10, “He has lunch at home” is not stated explicitly, and “He goes early to bed” depends on what “early” means. The practice key does not claim verified answers for those items.
  • Pupil’s Book page 11, exercise 10 ends with the open prompt “In the afternoon, I …”; no continuation is visible in the supplied image.
  • The printed Workbook time example says “7:00 o’clock”; that wording is preserved and documented without correction.

Grammar notes

1. The present continuous

Use am / is / are + verb + -ing for something happening now: I’m wearing a skirt. · She’s putting on a sweater. · We’re buying shoes.

2. The negative

Put not after am / is / are: I’m not wearing shorts. · She isn’t putting on a scarf. · They aren’t buying trousers.

3. Spelling the -ing form

The page prints: wear – wearing · do – doing · study – studying · eat – eating · buy – buying · put – putting · have – having · take – taking · swim – swimming · go – going. Most verbs add -ing; have and take drop the final -e; put and swim double the final consonant.

4. Present simple or present continuous?

always, usually, sometimes, never and every day go with the present simple: She always wears colourful clothes. now and Look! go with the present continuous: He is swimming now.

5. Punctuation

A full stop . ends a statement, a question mark ? ends a question, and a comma , separates items in a list: She’s putting on a shirt, a skirt and red shoes. · Are you buying new jeans?

6. Possession with ’s

Add ’s to a person to show possession: Maha’s bag is red. · Danny works on his uncle’s farm.

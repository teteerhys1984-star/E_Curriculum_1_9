By the end, you can…

  • name common rooms, home features and furniture;
  • describe a house using there is and there are;
  • make negative statements and yes/no questions about homes;
  • find details in a short description of a home and give your own description.

These are lesson objectives summarized from the supplied textbook pages; they are not additional book exercises.

Teacher guidance · source limitations
  • Pupil’s Book page 12 activities 1–2 and Workbook page 15 activity 4 require recordings that were not supplied. No audio answer keys or transcripts are provided.
  • The Pupil’s Book reading comprehension refers to “Linda,” but the reading passage does not name its narrator. The printed question wording is preserved and the source mismatch is documented.
  • The pronunciation box says “(silent letters). (h / e)” and highlights letters, but the supplied page does not give a clear item-by-item rule. The words are retained without inventing a pronunciation answer key.
  • Workbook page 14 prints one example for “Look and say what is wrong”; the remaining scene descriptions are open responses, not single-key answers.

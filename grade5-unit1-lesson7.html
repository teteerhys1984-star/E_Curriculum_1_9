GRADE 5 · UNIT 1 · LESSON 7

Hobbies

Talk about hobbies and free time, name hobby activities, use can and can’t to talk about ability, read about other children’s hobbies, and write about your own. Source material is separated by book and kept in original page order.

Pupil’s Book

Pages 24–26 · hobby words, Tala’s reading, and can / can’t.

Workbook

Pages 25–27 · Rana’s reading, picture description, a dialogue, listening tasks and can / can’t.

Vocabulary & Explanation

Hobbies, free-time activities and can / can’t, with Arabic meanings.

Lesson Test

18 independent questions and a separate teacher-answer-key area.

Learning goals

  • Name hobbies and free-time activities.
  • Read and understand a short text about someone’s hobby.
  • Talk about ability with can and can’t.
  • Ask and answer questions with Can you …?
  • Write sentences about what you can and can’t do.
  • Write a short paragraph about a favourite hobby.

Every exercise on the supplied book pages can be answered on screen. Where the book prints an answer, that answer is shown and marked as printed. Where the book prints no key, the exercise stores your answer and shows a suggested answer only where the page itself settles it. Listening tasks keep every printed instruction, picture and option, but no answer is invented and no audio file has been created.
